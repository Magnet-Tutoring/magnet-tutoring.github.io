Pricing

Choose private one-on-one help, a lower-cost small group, or a bundle of hours you can split between private and group sessions.

Free trial: your first 30-minute session is free, with one free trial per student. Book your free trial to come meet your tutor at no cost.

Rates and bundle

Example: a 1-hour group session for two siblings is $10 per person = $20 total. The group rate is per person, for up to 4 students.

Pre-book sale: pre-book the $70 bundle and save $10 and pay just $60 for your first bundle. Request the bundle to lock in the deal.

How payment works

Payment is handled offline. You pay on arrival at your session, by cash or Apple Pay. There's nothing to pay online when you book.

Cancellation & no-show policy: you can cancel anytime, though a heads-up at least 24 hours ahead is always appreciated. There's no charge if you miss a session. Because our tutors are students who only tutor a few hours a week, rescheduling may happen gradually, and we'll work with you to find the next available slot.

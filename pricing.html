How payment works

Payment is handled offline — you pay on arrival at your session, by cash or Apple Pay. There's nothing to pay online when you book.

Cancellation & no-show policy: you can cancel anytime, though a heads-up at least 24 hours ahead is always appreciated. There's no charge if you miss a session. Because our tutors are students who only tutor a few hours a week, rescheduling may happen gradually — we'll work with you to find the next available slot.

I am a 9th grader in the Wheeler Magnet program, and Spanish is the subject I love helping with most. Learning a language can feel overwhelming at first, with all the new vocabulary and verb endings, but I have found that it clicks once you stop trying to memorize everything at once and start using it a little at a time. I like making that process feel a lot less stressful.

When we work together, I will go at your pace and explain things in plain terms, whether you are just starting Spanish 1 or working through the subjunctive in Spanish 4. Bring your assignment or whatever you are stuck on, and we will practice it together until it feels natural.

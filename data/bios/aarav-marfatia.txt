Outside of the classroom I play both the piano and the cello, so I know what it feels like to practice something over and over until it finally clicks. Working through a tricky problem and seeing it come together is one of my favorite feelings, and I like helping other people get to that same moment.

When I am tutoring, my goal is to make the material feel a lot less intimidating.

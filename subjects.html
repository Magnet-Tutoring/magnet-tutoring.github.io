What to expect from a session

Here's what parents and students most often want to know before reaching out:

  • Who your tutor is: top students in Wheeler's prestigious Magnet program — peer tutors who recently learned this material themselves. Read more about us.
  • Session length: choose 30, 45, or 60 minutes, or grab the $70 bundle. See the pricing page for details.
  • In person, not online: we meet face-to-face at the Mountain View Regional Library (3320 Sandy Plains Road, Marietta, GA 30066). We're in-person only.
  • Ages and levels: grades K–12, from elementary subjects through high school math, science, English, and Spanish.

To make the most of your time: arrive on time, bring the assignment or materials you want to work on, and let your tutor know your goal for the session.

Cancellation policy: you can cancel anytime, but a heads-up at least 24 hours before your session is appreciated. There's no charge if you miss a session. See the pricing page for full details.

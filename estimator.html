Grade Calculator

Two quick, free tools to help you understand where you stand in a class. Everything stays in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

1. What's my current grade?

Enter each category (like Tests, Homework, Quizzes), the weight it's worth in your class, and your average score in it. We'll compute your overall grade.

2. What do I need on the final?

Enter your current grade, the grade you're hoping to finish with, and how much the final (or remaining assignment) is worth. We'll tell you the score you need.

For example, if the final is 20% of your grade, enter 20.

This calculator is an estimate for your own planning — your school's exact grading rules (rounding, dropped scores, extra credit) may differ, so always check your official gradebook. Stuck on the material behind the grade? Book a session and we'll help.
